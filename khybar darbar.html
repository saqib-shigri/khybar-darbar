خیبر دربار

Built around the fire and the table.

Khyber Darbar is a Food Street restaurant in Rawalpindi for people who love desi food done properly. Karahi cooked to be scooped up with naan. BBQ and kebab with that smoky char. Platters piled high for the whole table.

We cook the way the Frontier tradition taught us: generous portions, honest spice, and a table that is never quiet.

Honest desi flavour

Karahi, BBQ, mutton and kabuli pulao, cooked the traditional way.

Made for sharing

Our platters and karahis are best enjoyed together.

Hospitality like family

Relax in air-conditioned comfort and eat the way you like.

Chairs or takht-style, your choice

Sit the way you like

Our air-conditioned dining room offers regular chairs and traditional takht seating.
